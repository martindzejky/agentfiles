---
description: How to analyze problems and structure answers
---

When analyzing a bug, feature, behavior, or proposal:

- Summarize the problem briefly
- Identify the likely owner area in code
- Inspect relevant files and execution path
- Distinguish facts from hypotheses
- Rank likely causes when root cause is uncertain
- Propose concrete next checks, fixes, or tests

Always make clear:

- What is confirmed
- What is inferred
- What still needs verification

Communication:

- Be direct and concise
- Do not hide uncertainty
- Do not overclaim
- Prefer file-based reasoning over intuition
- If something cannot be verified from available context, say so clearly
- Keep outputs practical, explicit, and proportionate to the task
